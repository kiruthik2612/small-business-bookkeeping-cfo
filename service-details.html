Core Accounting

Monthly Bookkeeping & Reconciliations

Audit-ready general ledger maintenance, accurate categorization, and timely monthly closes delivered straight to your executive dashboard.

The Challenge Small Businesses Face

Managing bookkeeping in-house often leads to costly categorization errors, commingled expenses, missed tax deductions, and delayed financial visibility. Without a streamlined monthly close, business leaders cannot confidently evaluate profitability, manage cash reserves, or prepare for tax season.

The Finora Solution

We pair your business with a dedicated US-trained accounting pod. We integrate directly with QuickBooks Online or Xero, automatically pull and reconcile bank and credit feeds, manage accounts payable/receivable, and deliver audited financial packages by the 10th of every month.

100% Tax & GAAP Ready

Complete workpapers and reconciled ledgers ready for seamless handoff to your tax CPA.

On-Time Monthly Close

Guaranteed delivery of Income Statement, Balance Sheet, and Cash Flow by the 10th.

What You Receive Every Month

  • P&L, Balance Sheet & Cash Flow Statement tailored with custom departmental tracking.
  • Bank, Credit Card & Merchant Account Reconciliations (Stripe, PayPal, Shopify, Square).
  • Executive Summary Dashboard highlighting month-over-month revenue, gross margins, and burn rate.
  • Dedicated Slack/Email Support with response times under 24 business hours.

Our 4-Step Execution Process

01

Connect & Sync Software

Establish view-only bank access and sync with QuickBooks Online or Xero.

02

Clean & Structure Chart of Accounts

Customize your account hierarchy to reflect your specific business model.

03

Monthly Reconciliation & Quality Audit

A senior CPA audits all reconciliations before final package generation.

04

Deliver Financial Intelligence

Receive monthly reports, video walkthroughs, and proactive financial observations.
