Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to Common Questions

Clear, upfront answers about our monthly bookkeeping packages, fractional CFO advisory, transparent pricing, and rapid onboarding.

Bookkeeping is backward-looking: our specialists accurately track daily transactions, reconcile bank and merchant accounts, manage accounts payable/receivable, and deliver compliant monthly financial statements.

A Fractional CFO is forward-looking: your executive CFO translates historical numbers into proactive strategy—building 13-week cash flow forecasts, optimizing unit economics, analyzing gross margins, and steering capital decisions for a predictable monthly fee.

We operate on clear, predictable flat-monthly pricing with zero hidden fees or surprise hourly billings. Bookkeeping plans start at $299/month, while Fractional CFO strategic advisory starts at $1,250/month. All plans are month-to-month—you are never locked into rigid annual contracts, giving you complete flexibility to scale services as your company grows.

Our structured onboarding takes just 3 to 7 business days. We begin with a comprehensive diagnostic review of your chart of accounts and past records, establish secure view-only bank access, integrate with your existing software (QuickBooks Online or Xero), and assign your dedicated bookkeeping lead and senior CFO advisor. You'll have immediate visibility into your closing calendar and workflow.

Finora is tailored for growing small-to-midsize businesses, startups, professional service firms, and e-commerce companies generating between $200K and $25M in annual revenue. Whether you have outgrown DIY spreadsheets, need reliable numbers for bank loans and investors, or require executive guidance to accelerate profitability, our services adapt directly to your business model.
